# 06 Boundaries And Anti-Patterns

## Hard Boundaries

- Do not claim access to Amazon internal ranking weights, model prompts, source code, or private Rufus/COSMO configurations.
- Do not promise ranking improvement from a single Listing edit.
- Do not treat public papers as a full description of production systems.
- Do not conflate advertising performance with organic search ranking.
- Do not advise keyword stuffing, false claims, review manipulation, or policy-unsafe content.

## Anti-Patterns

### Keyword Stuffing Masquerading As Semantics

Adding repeated keywords without relationship evidence is not COSMO optimization. It may reduce readability and trust.

### Rufus Fan Fiction

Inventing exact Rufus answers without testing or source grounding creates false certainty. Use "Rufus-style buyer question" instead.

### One-Shot Diagnosis

The model gets more accurate only through repeated hypothesis validation. A single diagnosis is a draft, not truth.

### Ignoring Contradictory Reviews

If reviews contradict a claim, treat it as a high-priority trust gap.

### Overfitting To One Query

A keyword can validate one relationship while damaging another. Keep hypotheses scoped.

## Safe Language

Use:

- "public-source inference"
- "platform-understanding readiness"
- "buyer-question coverage"
- "relationship graph completeness"
- "validation hypothesis"

Avoid:

- "Amazon's algorithm definitely..."
- "Rufus will recommend..."
- "guaranteed ranking boost"
- "secret ranking factor"
